About

I recently completed my Bachelor's in Information Technology at Sree Vidyanikethan Engineering College, Tirupati. My research focuses on efficient multilingual NLP systems, particularly for under-resourced languages. I'm interested in developing language-agnostic model architectures that enable cross-lingual transfer learning without requiring massive computational resources.

My recent work addresses fundamental challenges in multilingual processing: how can we build AI systems that preserve linguistic and cultural authenticity while remaining computationally efficient? Through character-level and subword processing approaches, I explore scalable solutions for languages that lack extensive training data.

Publications

MultiChar: A Resource-Efficient Character and Subword Model for Multilingual Web Automation (Under Review at ARR)

Lakshmi Prasanna Pandeti. August 2024 - Present

Developed MultiChar, a resource-efficient neural framework for multilingual web automation that dynamically routes between character-level and subword-level models based on morphological complexity and task requirements.

Key Results: 83-89% precision in form filling, >90% in data extraction, 88-95% success in navigation across German, French, Arabic, and English

Technical Approach: Implemented a learned model selector (89.3% accuracy) and language-specific adapters requiring only 1.5% additional parameters per language. The system achieves 14.2% improvement over language-agnostic approaches.

Methods: Character-level MLM, SentencePiece tokenization, Transformer architectures, adapter modules

Status: Paper under review at ARR; Provisional patent filed in India

Efficient AI Resource Optimization using Structured Pruning and Fine Tuning on BERT and GAN Model (Accepted at ICONDEEPCOM 2025)

Kasarapu Ramani, Lakshmi Prasanna Pandeti, Thunga Reddy Sathvika. March 2024 - July 2024
